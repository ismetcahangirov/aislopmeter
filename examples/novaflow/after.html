The three emails it sends

They go out from your Gmail or Outlook, in your name, as replies to the original invoice email. You can edit every word before turning them on. Nothing goes out on weekends.

  1. Day 1 overdue

    To: accounts@lumenhotels · Re: Invoice 2026-041

    Invoice 2026-041 is now due

    Hi Mert, a quick note that invoice 2026-041 for the July photography (€2,400) was due yesterday. The payment link is below. If it is already on its way, please ignore this.

  2. Day 7

    To: accounts@lumenhotels · Re: Invoice 2026-041

    Checking in on invoice 2026-041

    Hi Mert, invoice 2026-041 is a week overdue. Could you let me know when I can expect payment, or who I should talk to if it is stuck?

  3. Day 14

    To: accounts@lumenhotels · Cc: you · Re: Invoice 2026-041

    Invoice 2026-041, two weeks overdue

    Hi Mert, this invoice is now two weeks late. I would like to settle it this week. Can you confirm a payment date by Friday?

    After this one, NovaFlow stops and hands the invoice back to you.

The one number we track

We looked at the 212 accounts that were active for all of August 2026. For every invoice that was already overdue, we measured the days from its due date to the Stripe or Paddle payment event, and compared it with the same accounts' invoices from the 90 days before they signed up. Invoices that were never paid are counted as open, not dropped.

What it will not do

  • Send a fourth reminder. Three, then it stops.
  • Text or WhatsApp your clients.
  • Add late fees, unless you write one into your invoice terms.
  • Email anyone from an address that is not yours.
