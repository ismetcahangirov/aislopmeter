# Kavruk design

The site looks like the bag the coffee comes in: kraft paper, black ink, a red date stamp.

## Colours
- Paper `#f5f4f0` (page background)
- Kraft `#c9ae84`, light kraft `#e9dcc6` (panels)
- Ink `#1d1b18`, secondary ink `#4a4540`
- Stamp red `#b3262a` (links, the roast-date stamp, hover)
- Rule `#d6d1c7`
- White `#ffffff` only for form fields

## Type
- Headings: "Archivo", condensed (font-stretch 75-80%)
- Body: "Literata"
- Dates, prices and labels: "IBM Plex Mono"

## Radius
Corners are square: 0px everywhere, like a paper bag.
