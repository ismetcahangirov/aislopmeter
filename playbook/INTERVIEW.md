# Questions that get real facts from the owner

Ask them in one message. Lead with the six most important; the rest are optional.

**Ground rules:**
- If the owner has no answer, cut the claim or leave a visible `[placeholder]`. Never fill the gap with something that sounds plausible.
- Save the answers in one file so numbers stay the same on every page.
- Show the owner the final copy before publishing.

## Always
1. In one sentence: who is this for, and what do they get that they cannot get elsewhere?
2. What do customers actually say when they recommend you? Their words, not marketing words.
3. Which three claims on the page could you prove today? For each: the number, the date, where it comes from.
4. Who is behind it? Names, roles, one true line of background, a photo you own, and how a visitor reaches a person.
5. What should stay exactly as it is: logo, colours, tone, sections?
6. Where are you reviewed or mentioned elsewhere? Google Business Profile, G2, Trustpilot, press, GitHub.
7. What is the registered company name, country and founding year?

## Software / SaaS
- Walk me through a new user's first 10 minutes: what do they click, and what do they see?
- How many paying customers or active users do you have exactly, as of what date, and from which system?
- Name three customers who agreed to be named. What did each achieve, measured how, over what time?
- What does the cheapest real plan cost? Billing period, limits, trial or refund policy?
- Which tools does it integrate with today, not planned?
- What does it deliberately not do? What does a named competitor do better?
- Which security or compliance certifications do you actually hold, and from when?

## Local business
- Full address, the area you serve, opening hours, phone.
- Your three best-selling items or services, with prices or price ranges.
- How long have you been open, and who does the work? Name one detail of your process only you would mention.
- Licences, insurance, memberships, with numbers or links.
- Describe a recent job: the problem, what you did, before and after photos, the cost range.

## Online shop
- What are the products made of, where, and by whom? Do you have photos you took?
- Shipping times and costs by region, the return window and who pays return shipping, payment methods.
- Real stock and lead times. Are any "limited" or "sale" claims tied to real dates and quantities?
- Real reviews: from which platform, how many, and can we link to them unedited?

## Portfolio / freelancer
- For three projects: the client (or a description they approve), your exact role, tools, timeline, a result you can measure.
- What work do you turn down? What is your minimum budget or day rate, and your availability?
- Which samples are entirely yours, and who else should be credited?

## Blog / publication
- Who writes here, and why should a reader trust them on this topic? Did an expert review it?
- For each key post: what did you test, measure or experience first-hand?
- Was AI used, and how (research, drafting, images)? Who checked the facts?
- When was each post last substantively updated, and what changed?
