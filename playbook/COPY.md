# Rewriting copy without making new slop

## Rules
1. **Say what it is, for whom, and one proof.** The hero names the category ("invoice reminders", "dental clinic", "adisyon programı"), the customer, and one concrete outcome.
2. **One claim, one fact.** A number with its source and date, a named customer, a price, a time, a step of the process. If the owner cannot supply it, cut the claim.
3. **The swap test.** Replace your brand name with a competitor's. If the sentence is still true, it says nothing. Rewrite it or delete it.
4. **Plain verbs.**
   - Use is, has, does.
   - Drop serves as, boasts, empowers, leverages, unlocks, elevates.
   - In Turkish, "sunmaktadır" becomes "sunuyoruz".
5. **No staging.** Cut "In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "Sonuç olarak", "Hiç … merak ettiniz mi?". Start with the point and end on a fact or a next step.
6. **No forced contrast.** Write "A CRM that logs calls automatically", not "Not just a CRM, it's a growth partner". Keep a contrast only when it corrects something the reader really believes.
7. **Real ranges and real audiences.** Do not write "From startups to enterprises" or "Whether you're a … or a …". Name who it is for, and who it is not for.
8. **Buttons name the outcome.** "See this week's menu" and "Book a 20-minute call", not "Get started" or "Learn more".
9. **Earned emphasis.**
   - Keep only a few bold phrases per page.
   - Use no emoji in headings.
   - Use em dashes rarely.
   - Do not end headings with "!".
10. **Cut, don't pad.** If a section has nothing specific left, delete the section.

## Before → after
Bracketed values are facts the owner must supply.

- **SaaS hero**
  - Before: "Unlock your team's full potential with our all-in-one, AI-powered platform. Seamlessly streamline workflows and supercharge productivity."
  - After: "Sprint planning, bug tracking and release notes in one tool for software teams of [5–50]. Replaces [Jira + Confluence] for most small teams."
- **Negative parallelism**
  - Before: "It's not just a CRM, it's your growth partner."
  - After: "A CRM that logs every email and call automatically, so reps stop typing notes after meetings."
- **About page**
  - Before: "Founded in 2015, Acme stands as a testament to our unwavering commitment to excellence, playing a pivotal role in shaping the industry."
  - After: "Acme started in [Izmir] in 2015. We have built [140] online stores, mostly for [textile exporters]."
- **False range and vague audience**
  - Before: "Whether you're a solo founder or a global enterprise, from onboarding to analytics, we've got you covered."
  - After: "For subscription businesses with [under 10k] customers. Handles billing, failed-payment retries and churn reports. No helpdesk or CRM."
- **Turkish hero**
  - Before: "Günümüzün hızla değişen dünyasında işinizi bir üst seviyeye taşıyacak yenilikçi ve kusursuz çözümler sunuyoruz."
  - After: "Kafe ve restoranlar için adisyon programı: sipariş, mutfak ekranı ve gün sonu raporu tek ekranda. Kurulum [1] gün sürer."
- **Turkish agency**
  - Before: "Biz sadece bir ajans değiliz; aynı zamanda büyüme yolculuğunuzdaki stratejik ortağınızız."
  - After: "[Ankara]'da [8] kişilik bir reklam ajansıyız. E-ticaret sitelerinin Google ve Meta reklamlarını yönetiyoruz."
- **Turkish corporate**
  - Before: "Alanında uzman kadromuz ve müşteri memnuniyetini ön planda tutan hizmet anlayışımızla sektörde öncü bir konuma sahip bulunmaktayız."
  - After: "Ekibimizde [6] mimar ve [2] statik mühendisi var. [2019]'dan beri [40] konut projesi teslim ettik; listesi Projeler sayfasında."
- **Closer**
  - Before: "In conclusion, the future looks bright as we continue our journey toward excellence."
  - After: delete it. End with "Send us your floor plan; we reply with a fixed quote within [2] working days."

## Testimonials
- Use only real quotes, with permission, and with the full name, role, company and date. Link to the original review if one exists.
- One specific quote ("Two clients paid the same afternoon") beats a grid of six generic ones.
- No real quotes yet: remove the section. Never generate them.
